# GUTTERLIGHT — Game Design Document

> *"Every light gutters. That is how it is passed on."*

A 2D hand-crafted-feeling metroidvania in the spirit of Hollow Knight: one large, interconnected,
non-linear underground world; tight, simple controls with real depth; melancholic atmosphere; a
story that is never forced on the player, only found. Everything — art, music, sound, story — is
original and generated by code at runtime. The game runs fully offline by opening `index.html`
(or the single-file build `dist/Gutterlight.html`).

This document is the **creative contract**. `docs/TECH_SPEC.md` is the **technical contract**.
When in doubt, follow these documents exactly; if something is genuinely missing, choose the option
most consistent with them and record the decision in your final report.

---

## 1. Pillars

1. **Feel first.** Movement and combat must feel tight and responsive: instant horizontal control,
   variable jump height, coyote time, jump buffering, crisp hit feedback (hit-stop, flashes, shake,
   particles, sound). Every action readable at a glance.
2. **Explore freely.** One continuous world of rooms. The player chooses where to go. Gates are
   ability-based and *visible before they are passable* so players remember places to return to.
   No invisible walls, no softlocks, no dead ends without reward.
3. **Story is found, not told.** No cutscene dumps. Lore lives in NPC conversations (which change as
   you progress), carved tablets, boss remarks, and hidden memories. Players who look will
   understand the whole tragedy; players who don't can still finish.
4. **Melancholy beauty.** Dark, quiet spaces lit by small warm lights. Strong silhouettes,
   parallax depth, drifting particles, soft music.
5. **Deep but simple.** Few buttons. Each ability has one clear use plus emergent uses.
   Sigils (charms) give build variety with simple, legible effects.

## 2. World & Story Bible

### 2.1 Setting
**Tallowdeep** is a kingdom of living wax buried beneath an ash-grey surface. Its people, the
**waxen**, are small candle-folk: bodies of wax, a wick for a soul, a flame for a heart. A waxen
lives as long as its wax lasts.

### 2.2 The truth (revealed gradually — never stated all at once)
- Long ago, at the bottom of the world, the **Chandler** — a vast, many-handed maker — found the
  **First Flame**, a living fire. From the warm wax that pooled around it (the **Mother Wax**), the
  Chandler poured the first waxen and lit each with a spark of the First Flame.
- **The Cycle:** a waxen burns its life; when its wax is spent it **gutters**, and its flame flies
  back down to the First Flame (**the Returning**). Fed, the Flame warms new Mother Wax, and the
  Chandler pours new waxen. Burn, gutter, return, pour.
- The waxen built Tallowdeep: the great cathedral-city of the **Nave**, the pouring-forges of the
  **Chandlery**, the wax-flower **Meltbloom Gardens**, the chimneys of the **Sootflue**.
- **Queen Lucerne**, the Lamplight Queen, most radiant of all waxen, came to fear her own
  guttering. With her three **Wardens** she built the **Everlantern**: a cage of glass and gold
  around the First Flame, to hold it perfectly still. Her decree: *"No flame shall gutter. No light
  shall end."*
- It worked — terribly. No waxen gutters now. But a caged flame can neither receive the Returning
  nor give warmth. Lights went out across the kingdom, yet the waxen did not die: their bodies kept
  walking, cold and empty, moved by a grey drifting smoke called **the Pall** — the flames that
  could not return. These are **the Snuffed**. From below, **the Rime** — a creeping cold — rose
  and began to freeze the deep places.
- The three Wardens sealed the way to the Everlantern with their own flames and withdrew:
  - **Abbot Tallowmere**, the Bell-Warden, in the **Drowned Nave**. He rings a great bell whose
    tone keeps the cold waxen docile and dreaming. He believes he is being merciful.
  - **The Kiln Mother**, the Forge-Warden, in the **Chandlery**. She poured an army of soldiers to
    guard the cage and still pours, endlessly, half-formed **moldlings** with no wicks at all,
    because she cannot stop.
  - **Duchess Vespertine**, the Wing-Warden, in the **Mothwood**. A great moth who loved the
    Queen's light above all things and swore never to let it fade.
- The Chandler, grieving, came apart. With its last strength it secretly poured one final waxen
  from the last drop of Mother Wax and lit it with a spark it had hidden before the cage closed:
  the Chandler's own **blue flame**. It left that small waxen on the ash at the top of the world.
- **That waxen is you: the Wickling.** NPCs call you "little wick" or "blue-flame". Your blue
  flame does not consume your wax; you cannot gutter… unless you choose to.
- **Sable, the Unlit**, is Queen Lucerne's daughter, poured from black wax and never lit. She
  guards the paths below. She knows the cage is wrong, but she fears what breaking it means:
  the end of everyone, her mother included. She tests you twice — first to see if you are strong
  enough, then (before the Sanctum) to see if you truly mean it. After the second duel she yields:
  *"Then burn, little wick. Burn for all of us."*

### 2.3 Endings
Defeat Queen Lucerne in the **Heartwick Sanctum**. Then:
- **Ending 1 — "The Undying Light"** (fewer than 6 Chandler's Sparks): your flame is not whole.
  You cannot break the cage. The Everlantern draws you in, and your blue flame becomes its new
  heart. The kingdom remains caged and cold; Sable keeps watch at the gate.
  *"And the light did not end. Nor did it shine."*
- **Ending 2 — "Gutterlight"** (all 6 Chandler's Sparks collected): the sparks rekindle the
  Chandler's fire within you; the Chandler's voice speaks one last time; you shatter the
  Everlantern. The First Flame roars free. The Snuffed fall still at last, their Pall rising as
  sparks that flow home. Tallowdeep burns down to its wicks. In the warm ash, many hands pour new
  wax; a small new light flickers. *"Every light gutters. That is how it is passed on."*

After either ending the slot stays marked complete (showing the last ending seen). **Continue on a finished save
resumes before the final fight** (at the last Sconce rested at, Lucerne alive again), so a player who saw "The Undying
Light" can gather the missing Sparks and win "Gutterlight" on the same save (Hollow-Knight-style).

### 2.4 Tone & writing style
Sparse, poetic, slightly archaic, never purple. Short lines. Humor is gentle and rare (Pell, Quill).
Characters speak in their own voices. No modern slang. Lore tablets are fragments — inscriptions,
decrees, prayers, graffiti — that contradict each other sometimes. Never name the "truth" bluntly
before the late game.

## 3. The Player: the Wickling

A small white-ivory wax figure, slightly tapered like a short candle, rounded shoulders, two tall
black eye-slits, a short black wick crowned with a **blue flame** (white core → cyan → deep blue
edge), a tattered ash-grey cloak/scarf trailing behind (2–4 segment verlet), small dark legs, two or
three wax drips down its sides. Carries **the Brand**: a slim dark-iron rod with a glowing hot tip.
Its slash arcs are white-blue crescents.

### 3.1 Controls (default; all bindings shown on the Controls screen)

| Action | Keyboard (both sets work at once) | Gamepad (standard) |
|---|---|---|
| Move / aim | Arrow keys **or** WASD | D-pad / left stick |
| Jump | **Z** or **Space** | A (0) |
| Attack (the Brand) | **X** or **J** | X (2) |
| Dash (Flare Dash) | **C** or **K** or Shift | RB (5) / RT (7) |
| Spell / Kindle (tap = cast, hold = heal) | **V** or **L** | B (1) |
| Interact (talk, rest, read) | **Up** / W when prompt shows | Up |
| Map | **Tab** or **M** | Back/Select (8) |
| Inventory / Sigils | **I** | LB (4) |
| Pause | **Esc** or **P** | Start (9) |
| Menu confirm / back | Enter, Z, Space, J / Esc, X, Backspace | A / B |

Attack direction: neutral/forward = horizontal slash; hold **Up** = upward slash; hold **Down**
while airborne = downward slash (**pogo**). Down + Jump on a one-way platform drops through it.

### 3.2 Movement (target feel — exact numbers in TECH_SPEC §8)
- Instant run (no slide), max run speed ≈ 8 tiles/s.
- Variable jump: holding Jump rises higher (up to ~4.6 tiles); releasing early cuts the jump.
- Coyote time (0.08 s) and jump buffering (0.12 s).
- Fast falls with a terminal velocity; soft landing dust; heavy landing after long falls.
- **Flare Dash** (ability): a quick horizontal burst of blue flame (~3.75 tiles, 0.18 s), no gravity
  during dash, works in air once per airtime, short cooldown. Damages nothing by default.
- **Wax Grip** (ability): touching a wall while airborne makes you cling and slide down slowly;
  Jump kicks off the wall (wall-jump); you can climb a single wall by repeatedly jumping off and
  steering back. Cannot grip **glazed** (`X`) walls.
- **Smokewing** (ability): a second jump in mid-air (puff of smoky wings), once per airtime.
- **Oilcloak** (ability): you can float and swim on the surface of meltwater instead of being hurt.
- **Molten Plunge** (ability, spell): slam straight down at great speed; shatters cracked floors
  (`D`); damages enemies on impact; invulnerable during the plunge.

### 3.3 Combat
- **The Brand** (nail): fast slash, ~0.38 s cooldown. Hitting an enemy knocks it back, pushes you
  back slightly (recoil), gives **11 Ember**. Down-slash bounces you up off enemies, spikes,
  thorn hazards and pogo-able objects (**pogo**), restoring your air dash and Smokewing.
  Brand damage by upgrade level: **5 / 9 / 13 / 17**.
- **Ember** (soul): a well that fills as you strike enemies (capacity 99 → up to 165 with 2 Ember
  Urns). Spend 33 to cast or to Kindle.
- **Kindle** (heal): hold Spell while standing still on the ground: after ~0.9 s, spend 33 Ember to
  restore 1 health. Can chain while held. Interrupted if hit.
- **Ember Bolt** (spell, ability): tap Spell to hurl a blue fireball that pierces enemies (15 dmg,
  +50% with *White-hot Core*). Costs 33 Ember.
- **Molten Plunge** (spell, ability): tap Spell while holding Down in mid-air. Costs 33 Ember.
  Impact shockwave 15 dmg (+50% with *White-hot Core*).
- **Taking damage**: lose 1 health (2 from some boss attacks), short freeze, screen shake, white
  flash, knockback, ~1.0 s invulnerability (flicker).
- **Hazards** (spikes, thorns, molten wax, meltwater without Oilcloak): 1 damage, fade out, respawn
  at the last safe ground position in the room.
- **Death**: you burst into wax and smoke; fade; respawn at the last **Sconce** you rested at with
  full health. All your **Cinders** stay behind in a **Gutterwisp** (a smoky, hostile echo of
  you) at the place you died. While a Gutterwisp exists, your Ember capacity is reduced by 33.
  Strike the wisp (3 hits) to reclaim your cinders and capacity. Dying again before reclaiming
  replaces the old wisp (its cinders are lost).

### 3.4 Health & resources
- **Health ("flames")**: start 5. HUD shows little candles; lit = health (blue flame).
  **Heartwax Shards**: every 2 collected = +1 max health (8 exist → max 9).
- **Ember**: shown as the **Ember Well** (a round glass vessel filling with blue-white fire).
- **Cinders**: currency. Dropped by enemies (2–20), found in **Cinder Geodes** (breakable clusters,
  30–120), sold to shops. HUD shows count.
- **Ember Urns** (2): +33 Ember capacity each.
- **Kiln Glass** (3): Brand upgrade material.
- **Chandler's Sparks** (6): hidden memories; each shows a short memory of the Chandler; all 6 →
  true ending.
- **Warden Seals** (3): Seal of the Bell (Tallowmere), Seal of the Forge (Kiln Mother), Seal of the
  Wing (Vespertine). All 3 open the Sanctum door.
- **Wax Notches**: sigil capacity. Start 3; +1 sold by Pell; +1 hidden (Guttered Ways); +1 from
  Old Thrum after you hold all 3 seals. Max 6.

### 4.1 Region connections & gating (macro)
- shore ↔ lastlight: open surface path (east–west).
- lastlight → guttered: **the Well** — a shaft from the village floor down into the Guttered Ways
  (the return climb must be possible without abilities: a stair of ledges/platforms).
- lastlight ↔ flue (chimney-tops, east of village): a **shortcut gate** opened by a lever on the
  Flue side (one-way shortcut, HK style).
- guttered ↔ meltbloom (west): through **The Effigy's arena**; its west gate opens when the Effigy
  is defeated.
- guttered ↔ flue (east): a broken bridge — a **dash gap** (≥ 9 tiles).
- guttered ↔ nave (down): a deep descent whose return climb needs **grip**; the entrance is gated
  so the player cannot drop into the Nave before having grip (e.g. tall climb up to the entrance).
- flue ↔ chandlery (down): the bottom of the Flue (needs grip within the Flue).
- nave ↔ chandlery (east): needs grip + dash.
- nave ↔ mothwood (west): a long flooded tunnel with a low ceiling — needs **oilcloak**.
- meltbloom ↔ mothwood: a shortcut lever gate opened from the Mothwood side.
- nave → rime: a cracked floor (`D`) — needs **plunge**.
- chandlery ↔ rime: a second route down, needs plunge.
- rime: internal traversal toward the Sanctum gate needs **wings**.
- rime ↔ sanctum: the **Seal Door** (needs all 3 seals) behind **Sable**'s 2nd duel arena.

### 4.2 Intended critical path (open-world allows variation after step 5)
1. Wake on the Ashen Shore → Lastlight → down the Well → Guttered Ways.
2. Find **Ember Bolt**; defeat **the Effigy** (opens west).
3. Meltbloom Gardens: duel **Sable** → **Flare Dash**.
4. Dash across the broken bridge → the Sootflue: defeat **the Soot Matron** → **Wax Grip**;
   open the shortcut to Lastlight.
5. Climb into the descent → the Drowned Nave: find **Oilcloak**; defeat **Abbot Tallowmere** (Seal 1).
6. (any order) Chandlery: **Molten Plunge**, **Kiln Mother** (Seal 2). Mothwood (via flooded
   tunnel): **Smokewing**, **Duchess Vespertine** (Seal 3).
7. Plunge into the Rime → use Smokewing to cross to the Sanctum gate → duel **Sable** again.
8. Open the Seal Door → **Queen Lucerne** → ending (depends on Sparks).

### 4.3 Collectibles placement guidelines
- **Chandler's Sparks** (6): one each in guttered, meltbloom, flue, nave, chandlery, mothwood —
  hidden (behind breakable walls, high ledges needing later abilities, end of optional branches).
- **Heartwax Shards** (8): 2 sold by Pell; 6 hidden across shore/guttered/meltbloom/flue/nave/rime
  (or mothwood).
- **Ember Urns** (2): 1 sold by Pell; 1 hidden in the Chandlery.
- **Kiln Glass** (3): flue, chandlery, mothwood (hidden-ish).
- **Sigils** (14): 5 sold by Pell (cinder_lure, smolder_shell, steadfast, waymoth, kindle_haste);
  the rest found: boss rewards / secret rooms / NPC gifts (Wren gives `long_brand` at her last
  meeting; Quill gives `waymoth`? — no: waymoth is Pell's). Exact placement in the world plan.
- **Wax Notches**: Pell (1), hidden in guttered (1), Thrum after 3 seals (1).
- **Cinder Geodes**: several per region in side areas.

## 5. Characters

### 5.1 NPCs (all waxen unless noted; dialogue changes with progress)
- **Old Thrum** (Lastlight): stooped elder, candle burned to a stub, beard of wax drips, walking
  stick, dim orange flame. Sconce-keeper. Hints and lore; notices your blue flame; gives a Wax Notch
  once you hold all three seals ("You carry their flames now. Carry this too.").
- **Pell** (Lastlight): round, cheerful peddler, huge pack of trinkets, a lantern hanging from it.
  Shop: Heartwax Shards ×2 (150, 450), Ember Urn (350), Wax Notch (400), sigils: Cinder Lure (100),
  Waymoth (120), Steadfast Wick (220), Smoldering Shell (280), Hastened Kindling (480).
- **Quill** (Lastlight): tall, lanky cartographer with ink-black hands and a quill tucked by her
  wick; hums. Sells **Charts** for each region (reveals unvisited rooms on the map):
  guttered 40, meltbloom 80, flue 80, nave 120, chandlery 120, mothwood 120, rime 150.
- **Bramm the Wickwright** (Lastlight): broad, leather apron, anvil, red flame. Upgrades the Brand:
  L1: 250 cinders; L2: 700 + 1 Kiln Glass; L3: 1500 + 2 Kiln Glass.
- **Wren** (wanderer): small traveler, wide hat made from a snuffer cap, walking pole, low flame.
  Met 4 times (guttered, nave, mothwood, rime). Her flame weakens each time; at the last meeting she
  sits by the frozen lake, nearly cold, and gives you her Longbrand sigil.
- **Sable** (non-combat appearances): glimpsed on ledges before her duels; after the 2nd duel she
  stands aside at the Sanctum gate.

### 5.2 Enemies (each region builder designs 3–4; all share the Enemy base)
Common family: **the Snuffed** — grey-blue cold wax bodies, slumped, extinguished black wicks
trailing grey Pall smoke, white pinprick eyes. Regional creatures add variety (moths, soot sprites,
moldlings, jellies). Enemies respawn when you re-enter a room (bosses and marked unique enemies
don't). Each enemy: clear telegraph before attacks, contact damage 1, distinct silhouette, death =
melts into a puddle + Pall smoke + cinder burst.

## 6. Places & objects
- **Sconce** (bench): press Up to light/rest: full heal, save, set respawn, sigil equipping.
- **Lanternway post**: light it once; then travel between lit posts (menu).
- **Lore tablet** (carved stone/wax slab): press Up to read.
- **Ability shrine**: a pedestal holding a floating blue ember; touching grants the ability with a
  full-screen card explaining the controls.
- **Pickups** (glowing): Heartwax Shard, Ember Urn, Kiln Glass, Chandler's Spark, Sigil, Wax Notch.
- **Cinder Geode**: hit several times; bursts into cinders; stays broken.
- **Breakable wall** (`B`): hit 3 times to shatter; hides secrets; stays broken.
- **Cracked floor** (`D`): only Molten Plunge breaks it; stays broken.
- **Lever**: hit with the Brand to open a linked gate (shortcut); stays open.
- **Gate**: iron/wax bars; opened by lever, boss defeat, or seals.
- **Boss arena gates**: close when a boss fight begins, open on victory.
- **Seal Door**: three sockets; opens when you hold all three seals.

## 7. Art Direction ("candle-noir")
- Dark, desaturated backdrops; strong silhouettes; warm small lights; parallax depth (3–4 layers +
  occasional dark foreground silhouettes framing the view); drifting particles everywhere; soft fog
  gradients; vignette.
- Terrain: organic, smoothed shapes (not visible square tiles). Dark interior fill, lighter rim on
  surfaces, region-specific texture & decoration (bricks, drips, vines, ice, metal plates).
- Characters: bold readable shapes with dark outlines; pale wax bodies; flames are bright glowing
  focal points. Animation through squash/stretch, bobbing, flame flicker, cloth sway.
- Effects: crescents for slashes, sparks on hit, wax chunks & smoke on death, rings for shockwaves,
  embers rising.
- UI: cream/white serif text with thin ornamental flourishes; dark translucent panels; minimal.

## 8. Music & Sound Direction
- All music is synthesized (Web Audio): felt piano, string pads, cello lead, celesta/bells (FM),
  choir pads (formant-filtered saws), harp plucks, sub bass, sparse timpani/percussion, global
  reverb. Melancholic, spacious, slow; boss tracks driving.
- **Leitmotif — "Gutterlight Theme"** (D minor, 4/4, 8th-note grid, 8 bars). Used by `title`,
  quoted in `guttered`, music-box version in `lastlight`, full in `ending`, inverted/darkened in
  `lucerne`'s phase 2:
  ```
  bar1: A4 - D5 - C5 A4 F4 -        chord Dm
  bar2: G4 - - A4 Bb4 - A4 -        chord Gm
  bar3: F4 - E4 F4 D4 - - -         chord Dm
  bar4: C4 - D4 E4 A3 - - -         chord Am
  bar5: A4 - D5 - E5 F5 E5 -        chord Dm
  bar6: D5 - - C5 Bb4 - A4 -        chord Bb
  bar7: G4 - Bb4 A4 F4 - E4 -       chord Gm | A (A7 on beat 3)
  bar8: D4 - - - - - - -            chord Dm
  ```
  (`-` = hold previous note one more 8th.)
- Tracks: `title`, `shore`, `lastlight`, `guttered`, `meltbloom`, `flue`, `nave`, `chandlery`,
  `mothwood`, `rime`, `sanctum`, `boss` (Effigy, Matron), `sable` (both duels; rival motif),
  `keeper` (three Wardens), `lucerne` (final, 2 phases: `lucerne` + intensity layer), `ending`.
- Ambience beds: wind (shore), village (lastlight), cave (guttered), garden (meltbloom), fire
  (flue), rain (nave), forge (chandlery), forest (mothwood), ice (rime), sanctum (sanctum).
- SFX: every action has a sound; enemy hits feel heavy; UI clicks soft.

## 9. Presentation & Flow
- **Title**: animated logo "GUTTERLIGHT" with a flickering blue flame and ash drifting; menu:
  Start / Continue (3 save slots with region, play time, completion %) / Options / Controls.
- **New game**: short verse intro (skippable), then the Wickling lies on the ash; its flame
  ignites; control returns.
- **Area title cards**: on first entering a region: large centered name with ornament + subtitle;
  later entries: small top-left name for 2 s.
- **Boss title cards**: name + subtitle at the lower screen as the fight begins.
- **Pause**: Resume / Options (music vol, sfx vol, screen shake, show controls hints) / Controls /
  Quit to Title (progress saved; you resume at the last Sconce).
- **Map** (Tab/M): visited rooms drawn as silhouettes in region colors; charted-but-unvisited rooms
  faint; icons for sconces, Lanternway posts, shops; your position (blue flame); pan with arrows.
- **Inventory** (I): abilities, health/ember stats, collectibles (shards, urns, kiln glass, sparks
  x/6, seals x/3), Brand level, and the Sigil grid (equip only at Sconces).
- **Completion %**: abilities (6), heartwax shards (8), ember urns (2), brand levels (3), sparks
  (6), seals (3), sigils (14), notches (3 extra), bosses (8) — each counts equally per item.
- **Ending**: illustrated text pages (procedurally drawn), then credits ("Made entirely in code"),
  then back to title. Save slot marked complete.
